How to Prepare for Your Spray Tan

  • Exfoliate gently before your appointment, following your technician's guidance.
  • Arrive with clean, dry skin and avoid applying heavy lotions or oils beforehand.
  • Wear loose, dark clothing after your session to help reduce rubbing.
  • Follow the product-specific rinse instructions provided by your tanning professional.
  • Moisturize regularly after your tan to help maintain an even appearance.

Why Choose a Custom Spray Tan?

A custom spray tan lets you choose the look you prefer without relying on UV tanning beds or intentional sun exposure. The experience should include clear instructions, attention to your preferences, and realistic expectations about color and how long it lasts.
