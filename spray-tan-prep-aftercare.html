Spray Tan Prep & Aftercare

Know what to ask before your appointment and how to follow product-specific instructions afterward.

Spray Tan Prep and Aftercare Guide

Preparing for a spray tan and following the aftercare instructions can help you understand what to expect from the service. If you are searching for spray tan preparation tips in Iowa City, start by asking the provider what its specific product requires. Instructions can vary, so general online advice should never replace directions from the tanning professional or product manufacturer. This guide is meant to help you prepare questions and plan your appointment.

A little planning can make the experience feel easier, especially if you are getting ready for a football game, formal, graduation photos, vacation, wedding, or another special event. Think about your schedule before booking. You may need time for the appointment, the product's development period, and the recommended rinse. Ask about all of these steps in advance rather than guessing based on a friend's experience.

Before Your Spray Tan Appointment

Plan Your Schedule

Choose an appointment time that gives you enough room to follow the provider's instructions. Ask how long the appointment usually takes, when you should rinse, and whether you should avoid certain activities during development. If you have an event, tell the provider the date so it can explain its product-specific timing recommendations. Do not assume every spray tan develops at the same speed.

Ask About Exfoliation

Many providers recommend gently exfoliating before an appointment, but you should ask when and how to do it for the product being used. Avoid irritating your skin or trying a new harsh product right before your session. If you have questions about shaving, waxing, or other skin-care routines, ask the provider how much time to leave between those activities and your appointment.

Arrive With Skin Prepared

Ask whether you should arrive with clean, dry skin and whether lotions, oils, deodorant, or makeup should be avoided beforehand. Recommendations may differ based on the formula and application process. It can help to bring loose, comfortable clothing for afterward. Dark clothing is often suggested because it may make any temporary transfer less noticeable, but confirm the provider's advice.

Share Questions and Concerns

Before the session begins, tell the technician the type of result you prefer and ask about the product. If you have sensitive skin, allergies, or ingredient concerns, discuss them before the appointment and review available product information. Ask what protective steps are recommended, including guidance about avoiding inhalation or contact with eyes and mucous membranes during application.

During and Immediately After the Appointment

Listen carefully to the technician's directions. Ask when you can dress, when you should rinse, and what to do if you notice uneven development or have questions afterward. Follow the exact rinse time and instructions provided for the product rather than using a general timeline from the internet. Avoid making changes to the recommended process unless the provider advises you to do so.

Wear clothing that follows the provider's guidance and avoid unnecessary rubbing while the product is developing. Ask whether exercise, sweating, water, or tight clothing should be avoided during that period. These details can depend on the formula. If anything feels uncomfortable or you are unsure what is normal, contact the provider for guidance instead of trying to fix the result with additional products.

Aftercare for an Even-Looking Result

Once you have completed the recommended rinse, follow the provider's aftercare instructions. Moisturizing regularly may help keep skin feeling comfortable and support an even-looking fade, but ask which products are appropriate. Avoid aggressive exfoliation unless you are intentionally removing the tan and have been given suitable instructions. Gentle skin care and following the product guidance are better than experimenting with several new products at once.

Ask how to manage showers, swimming, workouts, and skin-care products over the next several days. A spray tan is temporary and will gradually fade as the outer layer of skin naturally sheds. The way it fades can vary by person, skin care, and product. Do not expect a specific number of days or an identical result to another person's tan.

Quick Questions to Ask Your Provider

  • When should I exfoliate, shave, or wax before my appointment?
  • Should I arrive without lotion, deodorant, or makeup?
  • What should I wear to the appointment and afterward?
  • How long should the product develop before I rinse?
  • When can I shower, exercise, swim, and apply moisturizer?
  • Who should I contact if I have a question about the result?
